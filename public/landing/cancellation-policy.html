At Kitchen Network International Services Pvt. Ltd. (KNI), we strive to provide high-quality services and ensure customer satisfaction. This policy outlines the terms under which cancellations are processed across both our Services (AMC / Projects / Installations) and our Marketplace (Product / Equipment Orders). For refund details, see our Refund Policy.

Part A — Services (AMC / Projects / Installations)
1

Service Cancellation Rules

Service Orders

Once a service order is confirmed and work has been initiated, cancellation requests may not be accepted.

✓ Before Work Commencement

Customers may request cancellation within 24–48 hours of order confirmation, provided no work has been started.

◐ After Work Commencement

Cancellation requests will be reviewed on a case-to-case basis. Charges for work already completed, manpower deployed, or materials procured will be deducted.

✗ Emergency / On-site

Services booked for immediate or same-day execution are non-cancellable.

Part B — Products / Marketplace
1

Order Cancellation

Allowed

Before Dispatch

Within 24 hours of placing the order or before dispatch (whichever is earlier).

Not Allowed

After Dispatch

Once the product has been shipped, cancellation is not allowed.

Not Allowed

Custom Equipment

Customized or fabricated equipment cannot be cancelled once confirmed.

2

Return Policy

Returns are accepted only if:

  • ›Product is damaged during transit, OR
  • ›Wrong product is delivered.

⏱ Inform within 48 hours of delivery

Provide the following:

  • • Photos / videos as proof
  • • Order details
  • • Product must be unused, in original packaging, and with all accessories
3

Non-Returnable Items

✗Installed equipment
✗Used or damaged products (after delivery)
✗Custom-built / fabricated equipment
✗Perishable or consumable items
4

Shipping & Delivery

  • ›Delivery timelines are estimates and may vary.
  • ›KNI is not liable for delays caused by courier partners or unforeseen circumstances.
