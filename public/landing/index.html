About KNI

Kitchen Network International,

popularly known as KNI, is a comprehensive service marketplace designed exclusively for the commercial kitchen sector. KNI bridges the gap between restaurant and resort owners and kitchen equipment service professionals, offering a wide range of services to meet diverse operational needs.

It streamlines the management of service bookings, service calls, and tracking of service engineers, ensuring a seamless experience for businesses operating in the hospitality industry.

Our Vission

To be the leading platform transforming commercial kitchen operations by connecting businesses with reliable service professionals, driving efficiency, and promoting growth.
