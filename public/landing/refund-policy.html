At Kitchen Network International Services Pvt. Ltd. (KNI), we strive to provide high-quality services and ensure customer satisfaction. This policy covers refund eligibility, partial refunds, processing time, and refund modes — for both our Services (AMC / Projects / Installations) and our Marketplace (Product / Equipment Orders). For cancellation rules, see our Cancellation Policy.

Part A — Services (AMC / Projects / Installations)
1

Service Refund Eligibility

✓ Eligible Refunds

Refunds will be processed only if:

  • ›Service is cancelled within the allowed time frame, OR
  • ›KNI is unable to deliver the committed service.

✗ Non-Refundable Cases

  • ›Services already rendered
  • ›Custom orders / fabricated kitchen equipment
  • ›Advance payments for booked manpower or materials

◐ Partial Refunds

In case of partial work completion, refund will be processed after deducting:

  • ›Service charges
  • ›Material costs
  • ›Labour charges
Part B — Products / Marketplace
1

Marketplace Refund Eligibility

✓ Eligible Cases

  • ›Order cancelled before dispatch
  • ›Approved return due to damage or wrong item

◐ Partial Refunds

  • ›Deduction of shipping, handling, or restocking charges

✗ No Refund

  • ›For used products
  • ›For delays caused by logistics partners
2

Replacement Policy

In case of damaged or incorrect product:

  • ›Replacement will be provided subject to stock availability.
  • ›If a replacement is not available, a refund will be processed.

Refund Processing Time & Mode

Processing Time

7–10 working days

After approval, applicable to both services and marketplace.

Mode of Refund

  • ›Online payments → original payment source
  • ›Offline payments → bank transfer
